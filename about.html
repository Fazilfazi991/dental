About Dentiva

A shared dream. Built with passion. A clinic built around genuine care.

Dentiva began with a friendship between two passionate dentists who shared the same belief: dental care should feel personal, honest and reassuring.

What started as conversations about creating a better patient experience slowly became a shared dream to build a clinic of their own, where clinical excellence and compassionate care would always go hand in hand.

Our Story

Built from friendship. Guided by purpose.

Ruby and Habeeba first connected through their shared passion for dentistry. Over time, their friendship grew into a strong professional partnership, shaped by similar values, complementary strengths and a common vision for patient care.

They dreamed of creating more than another dental clinic. They wanted to build a welcoming space where patients would be heard, treatment would be clearly explained, and every decision would be made with their comfort and long-term wellbeing in mind.

That dream became Dentiva Dental Clinic, a practice created with friendship at its foundation and trust at the heart of everything it does.

Meet The Founders

The people behind Dentiva

Founder & General Dentist

Dr. Rubymol Ranjith

Dr. Rubymol Ranjith is a general dentist with extensive experience in preventive, restorative, cosmetic and family dentistry. Known for her attention to detail and patient-focused approach, she is committed to creating treatment experiences that are comfortable, transparent and tailored to each individual.

Her clinical interests include smile designing, root canal treatment, porcelain veneers, composite restorations, crowns and bridges, tooth extractions, minor oral surgery, paediatric dentistry and implant dentistry.

She holds a Bachelor of Dental Surgery and has undertaken advanced professional training in oral implantology, endodontics, veneers, clear aligner therapy and digital dentistry.

Founder & General Dentist

Dr. Habeeba Banu

Dr. Habeeba Banu is an experienced general dentist with more than seven years of clinical experience. Her approach combines skilled dental care with calm communication, helping patients feel informed and comfortable throughout their treatment.

She is experienced in comprehensive oral care, root canal treatment, tooth-coloured fillings, cosmetic dentistry, smile designing, simple and surgical extractions and preventive dental care.

A graduate of Anjarakandy Dental College in Kerala, India, Dr. Habeeba also brings strong organisational and leadership skills to Dentiva, helping ensure that the clinic delivers a smooth, welcoming and dependable patient experience.
